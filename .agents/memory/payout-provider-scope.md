description: Owner-confirmed provider, geography and requested payout currency.

Keep payouts manual, as explicitly chosen by the owner after provider review.
Use PesaPal only for this project. The owner asked to delete the Stripe
connection. The business and payout recipients are in Uganda; the requested
partner payout currency is USD.

**Why:** The owner explicitly stated, “Delete that stripe connection. I only use
pesapal,” and supplied Uganda and USD when asked about partner payouts.

**How to apply:** Do not infer permission to use Stripe from an installed
connection or add automatic transfers without a new owner request.
Confirm that the owner's PesaPal payout product supports Uganda
and USD, its fees, and its compliance requirements before integrating outgoing
payments. A requested currency does not establish provider support.
