---
name: Referral program privacy and financial policy
description: Applicant/founder access scope and why commissions require explicit founder confirmation.
---

Applicants must access only their own referral data; the founder can see all.

**Why:** The user explicitly required this while asking for the complete referral
workspace. The previous email-only public status lookup was not sufficient
proof of ownership.

**How to apply:** Keep ownership checks on the server, including legacy URLs,
exports and mutations. A visible or guessable affiliate ID/email is not
authorization. Keep founder-only notes out of applicant responses.

New commission rules require explicit founder confirmation; do not silently
choose a rate or backfill old payments as debt.

**Why:** The former public copy simultaneously advertised 30% recurring and
different flat plan commissions. The user has not chosen which financial
promise should govern. Historical payment totals alone do not establish what
was owed.

**How to apply:** Leave a new financial policy inactive until confirmed. Preserve
existing financial records; use policy snapshots for future commissions and
require a separate, explicit reconciliation decision for historical obligations.
