# Regional legal review and launch checks — 8 November 2026

Companion to [the policy review](legal-policy-review.md).
Working general legal information, **not** an opinion that every law listed applies,
a complete survey of every country, or a worldwide compliance certificate.
Country-specific counsel must validate scope and the actual processing before a targeted rollout.
The user explicitly requested coverage beyond the EU: ASEAN, Africa, GCC, China, Australia,
and North and South America. UK/EEA treatment remains included.

## How the documents handle these regions

The Privacy Policy now includes a linked, country/region-specific notice schedule.
The Terms preserve mandatory local protections despite choosing Ugandan governing law.
The Refund page distinguishes EU/UK withdrawal, Australian consumer guarantees, Brazilian
remote-purchase withdrawal, and other local remedies. It does not transplant the EU digital-content
exception or 14-day period into every country.

These notes are conditional on territorial scope, activity, thresholds and exemptions.
“Not applicable because we are small/free/open source/outside the country” requires a documented
assessment; it is not an automatic conclusion. Conversely, listing a statute does not mean
BrightBoard is automatically a regulated entity under every provision.

## Regional map and authoritative sources consulted

### Uganda / Africa

- Uganda: Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 and Regulations 2021; PDPO registration,
  rights, direct marketing, security, international processing and notification.
  [PDPO resources](https://pdpo.go.ug/information-center/resources);
  [Ministry Act](https://ict.go.ug/site/documents/Data-Protection-and-Privacy-Act-2019.pdf).
- Kenya: Data Protection Act 2019 and General Regulations 2021.
  [ODPC Act](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/TheDataProtectionAct__No24of2019.pdf);
  [General Regulations](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/THE-DATA-PROTECTION-GENERAL-REGULATIONS-2021-1.pdf).
- Nigeria: Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and current NDPC implementation requirements.
  [NDPC Act](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf);
  [NDPC FAQ](https://ndpc.gov.ng/faqs/).
- South Africa: POPIA, including electronic direct marketing, sensitive/child data and section 72 transfers.
  [Information Regulator](https://inforegulator.org.za/);
  [transfer guidance index](https://inforegulator.org.za/knowledge-base/category/popia/chapter-9-transborder-information-flows/).
- [AU Malabo Convention status](https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf)
  is not one permission for all African states. Other national laws, registrations, localisation,
  language and consumer requirements remain a per-market review.

### ASEAN

- Singapore: PDPA consent/accountability, purpose limitation, access/correction, retention,
  overseas comparable protection and relevant notification requirements.
  [PDPC obligations](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/data-protection-obligations).
- Malaysia: PDPA 2010 as amended by Act A1727 and current DPO/breach/transfer guidance.
  [Amendment](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Act-A1727.pdf);
  [2025 DPO guideline](https://www.pdp.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/GP_DPO_ENG.pdf).
  Do not use an old consultation paper as if it were final guidance.
- Indonesia: [Law 27/2022, government legal database](https://jdih.komdigi.go.id/produk_hukum/view/id/832/t/Undang-Undang%2BNomor%2B27%2BTahun%2B2022).
- Philippines: [Data Privacy Act 2012, NPC text](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/DPA-of-2012.pdf).
- Thailand: [PDPA 2019, regulator](https://www.pdpc.or.th/en/23134/).
- Vietnam: Law 91/2025/QH15, effective **1 January 2026**, and applicable implementing rules.
  [Ministry of Justice record](https://vbpl.moj.gov.vn/bocongan/Pages/vbpq-van-ban-goc.aspx?ItemID=179252).
- Brunei: Personal Data Protection Order 2025 and commencement notifications must be read together.
  [Commencement notification](https://www.agc.gov.bn/AGC%20Images/LAWS/Gazette_PDF/2025/EN/S%2011_2025[E].pdf).
- Cambodia, Laos, Myanmar and Timor-Leste need their own local checks. No absence-of-law
  conclusion has been made for any of them.
- [ASEAN/EU transfer-clause guide](https://asean.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/The-Joint-Guide-to-ASEAN-Model-Contractual-Clauses-and-EU-Standard-Contractual-Clauses.pdf):
  model clauses do not replace national scope, transfer and implementation requirements.

### GCC

All six countries require separate review, including applicable exceptions and sector/free-zone regimes.

- Saudi Arabia: PDPL, implementing regulation and overseas-transfer regulation.
  [SDAIA portal](https://scc-pdpl.sdaia.gov.sa/).
- UAE: Federal Decree-Law 45/2021, relevant implementation/exclusions; DIFC and ADGM are
  separate regimes, not all-UAE substitutes.
  [Official portal](https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/digital-uae/data/data-protection-laws).
- Qatar: [Law 13/2016, NCSA](https://assurance.ncsa.gov.qa/en/privacy/law).
- Bahrain: [Law 30/2018, official text](https://www.pdp.gov.bh/en/regulations.html).
- Oman: Royal Decree 6/2022 and Decision 34/2024.
  [Official implementing regulation](https://prod.mtcit.gov.om/ITAPortal//Data/English/DocLibrary/2024115131527120/Issuing%20the%20Executive%20Regulation%20of%20the%20Personal%20Data%20Protection%20Law.pdf).
- Kuwait: assess actual coverage under
  [CITRA privacy regulation](https://www.citra.gov.kw/sites/en/LegalReferences/Data_Privacy_Protection_Regulation.pdf)
  and relevant sector/user-protection rules; do not claim one GDPR-like omnibus applies identically to every entity.

### China

- Mainland PIPL, Data Security Law, Cybersecurity Law and current CAC implementation/transfer rules.
  [Official PIPL text](https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202108/t20210820_527244.shtml).
  Assess PIPL Article 3 territorial scope, Article 53 representation, sensitive data,
  under-14 guardian consent, required notices/consents, impact assessments and overseas-transfer mechanism.
- [CAC synthetic-content labelling FAQ](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654685896173.htm):
  measures/associated mandatory standard effective **1 September 2025**, where scope is met.
  Visible and metadata/implicit labelling are different; exact methods and agreement disclosure need
  to match an implemented technical system. No such system or required filing is certified here.
  Do not classify every purely procedural Canvas effect as an AI system without analysing the definition.
- Hong Kong and Macao have separate regimes. The drafts do not assert mainland rules cover
  those jurisdictions identically. Verify translations, local representation and restrictions before targeted offering.

### Australia

- Privacy Act 1988, APPs and Notifiable Data Breaches scheme where covered. Assess Australian link,
  exemptions and any exception independently rather than inferring exemption from current free pricing.
  [OAIC key concepts](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-b-key-concepts);
  [APP 8](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-8-app-8-cross-border-disclosure-of-personal-information).
- Australian Consumer Law can apply independently of Privacy Act coverage to overseas businesses
  supplying Australian consumers.
  [ACCC online selling](https://www.accc.gov.au/business/selling-products-and-services/selling-online).
  Do not treat a change-of-mind request as identical to a defective-service statutory remedy.

### North America

- US: state privacy/consumer law and covered-business thresholds; CCPA/CPRA notice, access,
  correction, deletion, opt-out, sensitive-information and non-discrimination where applicable.
  [California Attorney General](https://www.oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa);
  [GPC](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa/gpc).
  Assess AdSense disclosures even if private projects are not sold. Required opt-out/GPC recognition
  and state-specific appeal procedures have not been implemented or certified by the document change.
- [FTC COPPA FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions):
  distinguish adult uploads about children from collection from children; assess actual audience,
  knowledge and school context. Do not claim “FERPA certified” or that a school checkbox resolves every law.
  Review the amended Rule, not a historical FAQ alone.
- [FTC CAN-SPAM guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business):
  working opt-out, sender identification/address, suppression and applicable processing time.
- Canada: [OPC PIPEDA](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/pipeda_brief),
  relevant provincial regimes including Quebec; overseas accountability and any provincial impact assessments.
  [CRTC CASL](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/faq500.htm) can reach commercial messages sent into Canada
  from abroad; consent basis, sender identity and functioning unsubscribe are distinct requirements.
- Mexico: [2025 private-sector data law](https://www.ordenjuridico.gob.mx/Documentos/Federal/html/wo125102.html),
  ARCO rights, relevant notices, consent/transfer rules and current authority framework.
  Do not blindly retain old INAI references after the institutional/legal changes.
- Other North/Central American and Caribbean markets are not collectively covered by US,
  Canadian or Mexican law. Obtain country-specific scope/notice/consumer/transfer review.

### South America

- Brazil: LGPD and [ANPD international-transfer rules](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/assuntos-internacionais/international-affairs).
  Validate an actual applicable mechanism; EU clauses are not automatically Brazilian-approved equivalents.
  [Brazil Ministry of Justice Article 49 explanation](https://www.gov.br/mj/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/consumidor-tem-direito-ao-arrependimento-em-compras-on-line):
  generally seven days for qualifying remote purchases, with statutory start point and conditions.
- Argentina: Law 25,326 and [AAIP responsibilities/transfers](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip/datospersonales/responsables/obligaciones).
- Colombia: Law 1581/2012 and [SIC guidance](https://www.sic.gov.co/preguntas-frecuentes-pdp).
- Peru: Law 29,733 and current implementing regulation;
  [ANPD regulation information](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/anpd/campa%C3%B1as/128319-nuevo-reglamento-de-la-ley-n-29733-ley-de-proteccion-de-datos-personales).
- Uruguay: Law 18,331 and [URCDP rights](https://www.gub.uy/unidad-reguladora-control-datos-personales/politicas-y-gestion/conoce-tus-derechos).
- Chile: current Law 19,628; Law 21,719 reforms commence **1 December 2026**,
  after this review date.
  [Official text and commencement](https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/Navegar/imprimir?idNorma=1209272).
  A pre-commencement document must not represent the reform as already fully in force.
- Other South American markets need separate review. Do not describe LGPD as the law of the whole continent.

### UK / EEA

See the main review for GDPR, ePrivacy, EU AI Act, consumer and software-security sources.

## Additional publication gates for the expanded scope

1. Record actual target markets and establishment/representative details, applicable thresholds,
   local registrations, mandatory notices/languages and valid transfer mechanisms. No provider token
   or generic consent box proves these conditions.
2. Validate local child/student/sensitive-data requirements. Global under-13 exclusion alone does
   not satisfy China's under-14 rule or other countries' age/guardian obligations.
3. Add applicable consent, opt-out/GPC, request/appeal and complaint procedures. Do not force
   account creation for a qualifying privacy request merely because document sharing requires login.
4. Repair and test marketing unsubscribe/suppression across applicable CASL, CAN-SPAM, PECR,
   POPIA and other national regimes. Document exemptions where genuinely available.
5. Scope AI/synthetic-media duties beyond the EU, particularly mainland China, plus applicable
   US state and other local rules. Document provider/deployer/distributor roles. Decide SGR only after
   clarifying the acronym and the obligations it is intended to meet.
6. Validate mandatory local consumer remedies, taxes/invoice disclosures and cancellation/performance
   consents in actual checkout. The platform's fixed-period payment model and free Editor/API status
   do not waive privacy, accessibility, security or consumer obligations that otherwise apply.
7. Maintain a law-change review process, including Chile's December 2026 change. Do not rely on an
   English public policy or a static list of laws as the complete operational compliance system.
